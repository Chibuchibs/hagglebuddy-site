About HaggleBuddy

Built by Nigerians,
for Nigerians

HaggleBuddy was born out of a simple frustration: why do sellers always seem to know more about prices than buyers do? In Nigerian markets, information is power — and that power has always been tilted toward the seller.

We built HaggleBuddy to flip that equation. By crowdsourcing real prices from everyday shoppers and pairing that data with AI-powered coaching, we give every Nigerian the tools to shop with confidence — whether they're at Mile 12 on a Saturday morning or buying foodstuffs at Kaduna Central for the first time.

Our mission is simple: every Nigerian deserves to know the real price of things.
